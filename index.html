Synopsis

Risky Peaks follows Ogunda who is from a tribal village at the base of a dangerous and sacred mountain. When a mysterious sickness spreads throughout the village, the mountain becomes both the setting of danger and the symbol of hope. The theme centers around courage, responsibility, action and risk taking in order to save the village. Ogundas job is to save the villagers by climbing the mountain to gather a mythical plant that is said to cure the sickness that affects the village. Along the way Ogunda must fight against the ancient mountain spirit while climbing through different levels.

Ogunda Climbah

Age: young, 19 ish
Role: Chosen climber sent to retrieve the cure plant from the mountain peak.
Appearance: Ogunda wears fur-lined tribal climbing gear, a woven rope harness, and face markings symbolising protection. He carries a handmade climbing axe, a talisman from the village elder, and a small pouch for herbs.
Personality: Confident, observant, and empathetic. He is thoughtful under pressure. He relies on intuition and has a strong connection to nature.
Motivations: Save his village from the spreading illness. Honour the elders who chose him when all other climbers failed to return.
Goals: Reach the summit, retrieve the cure plant, navigate the mountain’s hazards, and avoid the mystical bird guardian. He must prove he is capable and worthy of the tribe’s trust.
Dramatic Need: To overcome self-doubt, trust his instincts, and accept fear as part of his journey.

The Mountain Guardian

Age: Ancient, ageless
Appearance: Mystical bird creature that guards the sacred (cure) plant at the mountain peak and transforms the mountain to test or repel climbers.
Appearance: A large, luminous bird with crystalline feathers that shift colour with the wind. Its wings create violent gusts capable of reshaping snow and stone. Its eyes glow with an intelligent, watchful presence. It appears both beautiful and intimidating.
Personality: Protective, territorial, and unwavering. The Mountain Guardian is not cruel but acts with strict purpose. It sees intruders as threats to the mountain’s balance. It communicates through environmental changes rather than speech.
Motivations: Preserve the sacred plant and maintain the spiritual balance of the mountain. Prevent unworthy or unprepared climbers from disturbing what it protects.
Goals: Challenge anyone attempting to reach the summit. Test their resolve through illusions, shifting terrain, and harsh weather. Ensure that only a climber with true spirit and intention may approach the cure plant.
Dramatic Need: To uphold its ancient duty as a guardian. Its purpose is tied to the mountain itself, and it cannot allow the plant to be taken without proving the climber’s worth.

Chief Aruka

Age: Late 50s to early 60s
Role: Leader of the mountain village who chooses Ogunda for the sacred climb. Serves as the guiding figure and emotional anchor for the story.
Appearance: Chief Aruka wears layered robes woven from dyed animal fibres, decorated with carved wooden beads and feathers symbolising wisdom. His long grey hair is tied back with ceremonial bands. He carries a staff etched with tribal markings representing past chiefs.
Personality: Calm, wise, and deeply spiritual. Aruka is patient and compassionate but carries a heavy burden as the illness spreads through the village. He speaks softly but with authority and is respected by all.
Motivations: Protect the village and preserve its traditions. Ensure that the cure plant is retrieved by someone spiritually aligned with the mountain.
Goals: Guide Ogunda, offer blessings and knowledge, and prepare him mentally for the journey. Maintain hope in the village despite previous failed attempts.
Dramatic Need: To trust the new generation and accept that he cannot protect the village alone. He must let go of the fear of losing another climber.

Temba the Wanderer

Age: Unknown (appears mid 40s but could be much older)
Role: A mysterious travelling merchant who appears at the end of each level to sell upgrades, tools, charms, and survival items in exchange for earned XP.
Appearance: Temba dresses in layered travelling robes stitched from many different cultures’ fabrics. He carries a pack overflowing with trinkets, glowing stones, rope coils, dried herbs, and odd mechanical gadgets. His eyes have a faint shimmer that suggests he knows far more than he lets on.
Personality: Warm, talkative, and mischievous. Temba speaks in riddles and jokes but always gives the player exactly the hint they need. He seems to appear and disappear without leaving footprints, as if the mountain itself allows him to pass.
Motivations: To help worthy climbers survive the mountain’s trials. He trades goods not for profit but to ensure that the right person reaches the summit.
Goals: Offer equipment upgrades, restorative items, charms that weaken the mountain’s illusions, and tools that open optional paths. Encourage the player to keep pushing upward through the challenges.

Elder Tohu

Role: A miniature spectral projection of Elder Tohu, an ancient former chief of the village who was once a legendary climber. He appears during the early levels to guide the player, teach controls, and offer hints. His knowledge of the mountain comes from his own past ascent many generations ago.
Appearance: Elder Tohu appears as a tiny, blue, glowing spirit figure with simplified, exaggerated expressions that make him easy to read during gameplay. His body is formed from soft swirling mist, and he floats effortlessly above the ground. His shortened spirit-staff acts as a pointer for highlighting hazards or important mechanics. A faint mist trails behind him, giving him an ethereal but friendly presence.
Personality: Comedic, expressive, and slightly dramatic. Elder Tohu gestures wildly when explaining mechanics and uses humour to make the player feel at ease. Beneath his playful exterior he carries deep wisdom about the mountain, its trials, and the nature of the Mountain Guardian. He enjoys guiding new climbers and takes pride in passing on the lessons he learned during his own ascent.
